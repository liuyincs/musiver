CarPlay is a **membership** feature on **iPhone** (not iPad).

1. Unlock membership and add a working server on the phone.
2. Connect CarPlay and open Musiver.
3. Browse / play from the car interface.

Prepare a reachable remote connection or offline downloads before driving; a home-LAN-only address may fail on the road.
