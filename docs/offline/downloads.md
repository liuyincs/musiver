title: "Downloading music and automatic downloads"

Use an installed client that supports downloads. The current Web app does not support formal audio downloads.

## Download songs

1. Open a song's action menu and choose **Download**.
2. Open **Downloads** to inspect the tasks.
3. Wait until the task completes. Pending, paused, or failed tasks are not ready for offline playback.
4. Open the downloaded content and test playback.

The task list offers pause, resume, retry, and cancellation. If a task will not resume, check **Background Download Policy**. On Android, its choices include **Wi-Fi Only**, **Always Allow**, and **Paused**.

## Automatic downloads

On supported clients, open **Settings → Auto Download** with a server active. The feature requires membership where indicated.

Select favorite tracks, frequently played songs, or specific playlists, then choose the download quality and available transcoding format. On Android, you can also set the number of frequently played songs to include. Check the task list after configuration to confirm what is being downloaded.

Automatic downloading depends on network policy and the app being allowed to run. It is not a guarantee that a newly added song is already available offline. See [offline playback](listening.md).
