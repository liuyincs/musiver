---
title: "Listening offline"
sidebar_position: 2
---

Prepare audio before leaving the network. Seeing a song in a library or having played part of it does not prove the complete audio is available locally.

1. Finish the [download tasks](downloads.md) for the music you need.
2. Open **Downloads** and choose the offline content view.
3. Play the downloaded tracks. Before a trip, test them without a network connection.

Android switches to local browsing according to network status; you do not need to find a manual offline-mode switch. Opening downloaded content directly also helps distinguish available audio from online-only library entries.

Server search, editing remote playlists, and other server-dependent actions may be unavailable offline. Downloaded audio and lyrics are separate: check that needed lyrics have been fetched before disconnecting.

Playback cache can also contribute local audio, but [cache cleanup](storage.md) can remove it. Use explicit downloads for the music you intend to keep for a trip. The Web app does not provide the same download-based offline library as installed clients.
