---
title: "Favorites and listening history"
sidebar_position: 4
---

Use the favorite action on a song or in the player to mark music you want to return to. Open **Favorites** or **My Favorites** to browse the items supported by your server.

Favorites and playlists are separate. Favoriting a song does not add it to a named playlist. Neither action means the audio is already downloaded; configured automatic-download rules can download it afterward.

Open **Play History** to revisit the playback history recorded by Musiver for the current server and library. It does not collect listening history from other players and is not a backup of your collection.

To keep favorites available offline, [download them](../offline/downloads.md) or configure automatic downloads on a supported client. To discover related music while listening to favorites, see [music roaming](../playback/modes.md).

If the favorite button is missing or cannot be changed, check the current server's capabilities and whether you are online.
