---
title: "Songs, albums, and artists"
sidebar_position: 2
---

Use **Songs**, **Albums**, or **Artists** to browse the current library in different ways.

- Open **Songs** when you want to choose an individual track.
- Open an album to see its track order and play its contents.
- Open an artist to explore the albums and tracks returned by your server.

Use the list's sort and filter controls where available. The available choices depend on the server and the current view.

Choosing a song to play and adding it to the queue are different actions. To keep your listening order, use the song menu's **Play Next** or **Add to Queue** instead of starting another list. See [player and queue](../playback/player-queue.md).

Missing artwork, duplicate artists, and incorrect album grouping often originate in server metadata. Check the same item in the server's own interface before changing Musiver settings.
