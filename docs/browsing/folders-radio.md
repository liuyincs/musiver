## Genres and folders

Open **Genres** to browse music by the server's genre tags. Open **Folders** to follow the folder structure exposed by the server. Folder browsing is not access to arbitrary files on your device.

If a genre or folder is missing, check the server's tags, scan status, and account permissions. Some services do not expose these views through their API.

## Radio

Open **Radio** and choose a station provided by the server. A station is a live stream rather than a finite song, so track seeking, duration, and song-based actions may not apply.

If a station fails while normal songs play, test its stream on the server side. The station URL may be unavailable or use a format the playback device cannot handle. The sleep timer can stop a radio stream, but does not offer the option to finish the current song.
