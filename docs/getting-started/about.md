Musiver is a player for audio you keep on a server. Your server stores the files and provides your library; Musiver signs in, lists the content your account can access, and plays it.

## What you need

- A running server: Navidrome, Subsonic, Audio Station, Emby, Jellyfin, Plex, or Audiobookshelf.
- Its address and your login details. Plex uses browser authorization.
- A network connection from your device to the server. An address that works at home may not work outside your home network.

If someone else manages the server, ask them for the server type, connection address, and your account. Installing Musiver does not install or configure the server.

You can browse music, build a playback queue, use supported playlist features, and download audio on supported clients. Audiobooks and podcasts have [separate playback settings](../spoken-audio/playback.md).

See [servers, libraries, and connections](../servers/concepts.md) before configuring more than one library or address.
