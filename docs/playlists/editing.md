Playlists belong to the current server. Available actions depend on the service and whether your account owns or can edit the playlist.

## Create a playlist

1. Open the menu beside your playlists and choose the new-playlist action.
2. Enter a name and complete creation.
3. Open a song's action menu, choose the add-to-playlist action, and select your playlist.
4. Open the playlist to check its contents.

Use the playlist's more menu to rename or manage it where supported. Removing a song from a playlist changes the playlist, not the underlying audio file.

A shared playlist can be visible without being editable. Server-generated smart playlists are also different from ordinary playlists: their rules determine their tracks, so direct additions and removals are unavailable.

To preserve your current queue, use its **Save as Playlist** action where offered. To save a list outside the app, see [playlist export](ordering-export.md). For importing into Tag My Audio, use the [experimental import guide](../experimental/playlist-import-transfer.md).
