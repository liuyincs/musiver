## What it is

Lyrics can come from the file, the server, or a [custom API](../settings/custom-api.md). Musiver supports word-by-word timing, immersive display, overlays, and built-in lyric search.

## How to use

1. Open the player to view lyrics when available.
2. Use lyric search from the player menu; preview a candidate and apply it (saved locally).
3. Adjust font / alignment in lyric settings.
4. Enable platform overlays (notification, status bar, floating, PiP) where your client offers them.
