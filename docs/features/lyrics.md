## What it is

Lyrics can come from the file, the server, or a [custom API](../settings/custom-api.md). When timing is available, word-by-word lyrics highlight as you sing along. Musiver also supports immersive display, desktop / floating / status / PiP overlays, and built-in lyric search.

## How to use

1. Open the full player to view lyrics when available.
2. Use lyric search from the player menu; preview a candidate and apply it (saved locally).
3. Adjust font and alignment in player Preferences → Lyrics.
4. Enable platform overlays (notification, status bar, floating, PiP) where your client offers them.

## Membership / platform

Viewing and searching lyrics in the player, and player bar lyrics, are free. Immersive, notification, status bar, floating, and PiP lyrics require membership per [plans](../membership/plans.md).
