## What it is

The player bar and full player control playback, queue order, audio quality, equalizer, ReplayGain, sleep timer, and output — including free DLNA cast.

## How to use

1. Start a song, then open the full player from the playback bar.
2. Build order with Play Next / Add to Queue; starting another list may replace the queue.
3. Choose online quality: lossless or 320 / 256 / 192 / 128 kbps transcode (server permitting).
4. Open equalizer / ReplayGain / music roaming from the player menu or settings where shown.
5. Set a sleep timer (optionally finish the current track first).

![Player menu with Equalizer](/img/docs/features/desktop-equalizer-menu.jpg)

## Sleep timer

Use the sleep timer when you want playback to stop after a set time, or after the current track ends. It is available from the player controls on supported clients.

## DLNA casting

DLNA is free in Musiver. From the playback bar, open Cast, refresh the device list, pick a DLNA device on your LAN, then switch back to local output when you are done. Equalizer is unavailable while casting. Web has no DLNA.

![Cast / DLNA](/img/docs/features/desktop-dlna-cast.jpg)
