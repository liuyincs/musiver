---
title: "Appearance and personalization"
sidebar_position: 1
---

Open **Settings** and find the **Appearance** section to choose the available light/dark appearance and theme colors. Use **Personalization** for the layout and display options offered by your client.

1. Choose the appearance you want to use.
2. Open personalization and adjust the Discover page, playback bar, or player background as needed.
3. Open lyric settings for lyric presentation, then play a song to check the result.

The available controls differ between phone, desktop, and TV layouts. A desktop background option may not have an identical control on a phone.

If text or artwork becomes difficult to read, return to appearance settings and choose a simpler background or a different contrast. Change one option at a time so you can identify the setting responsible.

Appearance settings affect the client display. They do not edit album artwork or metadata stored on the server. For incorrect song information, check the server's library instead.
