title: "Migrating from the old version"
sidebar_position: 3
---

Musiver and the old StreamMusic are two different apps: the package name changed from `cn.aqzscn.stream_music` to `cn.musiver.app`, and both can be installed side by side without affecting each other.

Treat Musiver as a **fresh install** — the old app's servers, preferences, downloads, and cache cannot be imported. The only thing that carries over is your **membership identity** (bind an email in the old app, then restore the purchase in the new one).

## Before you switch

1. Record server type, addresses, and account details.
2. In the old app, bind an email to your membership before you consider removing that app.

## Set up Musiver

1. [Install](../getting-started/install.md) and [add your server](../getting-started/first-play.md).
2. Verify browsing and playback on the same library.
3. Open Restore purchase and use the email + verification code bound in the old app to move your membership over.

The two versions can coexist; keep the old app until the new setup meets your needs. Membership migration details: [purchase & restore](../membership/purchase-restore.md).
