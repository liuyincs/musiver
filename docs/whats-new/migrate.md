Treat Musiver as a **new install**. Automatic import of the old app’s servers, preferences, downloads, and cache is **not** established.

## Before you switch

1. Record server type, addresses, and account details.
2. In the **old** app, bind an email to your membership before you remove that app.
3. Note settings and offline tracks you need to recreate.

:::caution TODO screenshot: old StreamMusic app — membership email binding screen
:::

## Set up Musiver

1. [Install](../getting-started/install.md) and [add your server](../getting-started/first-play.md).
2. Verify browsing and playback on the same library.
3. Open **Restore purchase** and use the **email + verification code** bound in the old app.
4. Re-download offline music in the new client and test offline.

![Privacy confirmation on first launch (desktop)](/img/docs/getting-started/desktop-privacy-modal.jpg)

## What does **not** transfer

- Local downloads / cache from the old app
- QR “sync data between devices” as an import path into Musiver
- Library-mode local mirrors (feature not implemented yet)

Keep the old app until the new setup meets your needs. Details: [purchase & restore](../membership/purchase-restore.md).
