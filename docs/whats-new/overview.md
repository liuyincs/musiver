The new Musiver uses native technology on most platforms for better performance and experience.

### New platforms

Adds tvOS, Linux, HarmonyOS, and self-hosted Web — from the living-room TV to the browser.

![Platforms](/img/docs/illustrations/platforms.svg)

See [TV](../devices/tv.md) and [Web](../devices/web.md).

### Word-by-word lyrics

Lyrics sweep across word by word in time with the melody.

![Word-by-word lyrics](/img/docs/features/iphone-lyrics.png)

See [Lyrics](../features/lyrics.md).

### Equalizer

A 10-band equalizer built into the player — together with ReplayGain and music roaming, a new sound toolkit (membership).

![Equalizer](/img/docs/features/iphone-equalizer.png)

See [Player](../features/player.md).

### Plex OAuth

Plex servers now sign in with official OAuth authorization, including Plex Home members and PIN switching.

![Plex OAuth sign-in](/img/docs/features/iphone-plex-oauth.png)

See [Plex](../servers/plex.md).

### Audiobookshelf

New audiobook server support: bookshelf / series / author browsing, synced progress and resume, chapters and playback-speed memory.

![Audiobookshelf](/img/docs/features/iphone-audiobookshelf.png)

See [Audiobookshelf](../servers/audiobookshelf.md).
