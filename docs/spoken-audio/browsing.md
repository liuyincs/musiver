---
title: "Browsing books, series, and authors"
sidebar_position: 2
---

Select an audiobook library before looking for its specialized navigation. Supported servers such as Audiobookshelf can provide shelf, series, and author views.

1. Open the shelf to choose a book.
2. Open a series to browse related books, or an author to explore their works.
3. Open the book's details and start playback.

For podcasts, select the podcast library and browse the available episodes. The exact grouping depends on the server's content and metadata.

If these views do not appear, first check the current library and its [type](library-types.md). Selecting **All Libraries** across mixed content is different from selecting a specific audiobook library.

Setting an ordinary music library to audiobook enables appropriate playback treatment, but does not give its server Audiobookshelf's browsing capabilities. Correct missing series, author names, and chapter metadata in the server rather than expecting the type setting to generate them.
