Open the player to view lyrics when available. Lyrics may come from the audio file, the server, or a configured [custom API](../settings/custom-api.md).

## Find a different match

1. Open the player's more menu and choose lyric search.
2. Enter the song title, artist, or album. Choose the server or a configured custom source.
3. Inspect the results and preview a candidate.
4. Select **Use This Lyric** to apply your choice.

Your selected lyrics are saved locally and are not replaced by a later remote refresh. A custom source is unavailable until its API is configured.

Use lyric settings to adjust the display options offered by your client, such as font size, alignment, and the appearance of unfocused lines. These settings change presentation, not the audio file.

Cached and embedded lyrics can be read offline. Downloading audio attempts to fetch lyrics, but a lyric-fetch failure does not fail the audio download. Check lyrics before disconnecting if you need them offline.
