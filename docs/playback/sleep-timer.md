---
title: "Sleep timer"
sidebar_position: 5
---

Use the player's sleep timer when you want playback to stop later.

1. Open the player's more menu and choose the sleep timer.
2. If offered, enable the option to finish the current track after the timer expires.
3. Choose a duration: 15, 30, 45, 60, or 120 minutes.
4. Reopen the timer menu to check the remaining time or cancel it.

With finish-current-track enabled, playback can continue past the chosen duration until the track ends. For a long audiobook chapter, that may be much later than the timer time. Leave the option off when you need playback to stop at the chosen time.

Radio streams do not offer finish-current-track because they have no normal track ending. Use the timed stop instead.
