Start a song, then open the player from the playback bar. Use the playback controls to pause, resume, change tracks, and seek within a track. Live radio does not have the same seeking behavior as a normal song.

## Build your listening order

- **Play Next** puts a selected song next in the queue.
- **Add to Queue** appends it for later listening.
- Starting playback from another list can replace the current queue.

Open the queue to inspect what will play next. Select an item to jump to it, remove unwanted items, or clear the queue. Removing an item from this queue does not delete the server's audio file.

Where available, **Save as Playlist** turns the queue into a playlist on the current server. This depends on playlist creation support and does not download the songs.

Use [playback modes](modes.md) to change how the queue is played. For books and podcasts, [long-audio controls](../spoken-audio/playback.md) can replace previous/next with skip backward/forward.
