title: "Audio quality, equalizer, and ReplayGain"
sidebar_position: 4
---

These settings solve different problems. Online quality controls the stream requested from the server; the equalizer changes its tonal balance; ReplayGain uses gain metadata to reduce loudness differences.

## Online quality

Open **Online Audio Quality** in settings and choose the quality and transcoding format offered for your server. Desktop has a single online-quality row; mobile clients may distinguish network types. A route-specific quality setting can override the general choice.

Lower quality can reduce network use, but requires compatible server transcoding. If a selected format fails, try another offered format or original quality and check the server's transcoding setup. This does not change existing downloads.

## Equalizer

Open the equalizer from the player, enable it, and choose a preset or adjust the frequency bands. The feature requires membership and is unavailable during casting. Adjust playback volume carefully after boosting bands.

## ReplayGain

On supported installed clients, open ReplayGain settings, enable it, and choose album or track preference. Album preference is useful when preserving relative levels within an album. You can adjust preamp gain or reset that gain to 0 dB.

ReplayGain depends on available gain information and does not guarantee identical loudness for every song. The Web app does not support it. These playback settings do not rewrite your server's files.
